Motion
It sees: Walking, running, and nobody.

It calls you:
- Unhurried: Motion detected and held for 6 seconds.
- Rushing: Motion detected and held for 2 seconds.
- Nobody: No motion detected, or the person is over 130 cm away for 5 seconds.
It does:
- Unhurried: Displays a slow animation on the OLED.
- Rushing: Displays an alert on the OLED.
- Nobody: Turns the OLED off.
For them: It gives you an idea of how quickly you are moving.

To them: It labels you based on how quickly it thinks you are moving.

It refuses: To reveal the distance it measures, even though it knows it.

Set aside: Loud beeping sounds and notifications.

Sits next to: Flock cameras or Hansel and Gretel.

The tension: Being tracked and given a label that might not accurately represent who you are or what you are doing.

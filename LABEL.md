# Motions 
###### By: David Laredo, Henry Tolar

## Materials 
1. Oled screen 
2. Bread board
3. PIR motion sensor
4. Male - Female wires

## Notices
It notices your movement and how fast you move. It calls you to move quickly, kinda quickly, or not at all in order to present a label. Then it tells you what your label is on the screen no emotion. It refuses to break motion and it refuses to add emotions to the labels.
